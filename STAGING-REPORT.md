**What this is:** the story underneath THE ROOM (design `docs/research/room-codec/04-story-generator.md`, `01-personas.md`, `02-the-queen.md`, resting on `story-lore-research-2026-10-14.md`). Two personas now keep score of each other from real events — verdicts, crowns, blooms, keeps, proposals — on two timescales each (a fast mood over a slow standing). A beat selector turns that state into episodes with five phases and a strict peak quota, under the research's eight anti-repetition rules, silence included. The room itself is untouched: no transcript line is invented, no recorded decision shape changes, and the arc has no readout anywhere — not even backstage.

## The story state: shape and home

- **`src/curator/storyState.js`** (new, pure — no clock, no storage, no randomness of its own; rolls are injected) holds the whole machine:
  - **The book** (persisted): session count; two directed standings — *esteem* (LOIS→Davis: her regard for him) and *trust* (Davis→LOIS: his reliance on her judgment) — each a `{ mood, standing }` pair; per-persona **believed** standings (what each currently takes the standings to be); **grudge records** per topic (offence count, praise count, promoted flag); the **suspicion record** (value, last-increment session, armed milestone, anomaly count); per-kind **form bags** (anti-repetition state).
  - **The episode** (per session, not persisted): phase, intensity, bar cursor, the patterns sifted so far (streaks, reversals, debts, promotions, the misread), cooldowns, the session's story-beat count and peak count, and `lastWorkSeq` — the ledger position of the last piece of real work, which the arc's verification depends on.
  - **Persistence:** versioned JSON, fail-closed decode (any corrupt section falls back on its own; junk decodes to a fresh book — proven by test). Sessions are driver runs (app boots), matching the engagement/presence precedent.
- **`src/curator/storyLive.js`** (new) is the live half, built exactly like `mindPresenceLive`: it owns storage (key **`kc:room:story:v1`**, saved every 16 folded records and on stop), subscribes to the real bus (MIND_DECISION, DIRECTOR_ROOM, DICE_CAST, BLOOM, KEEP_ADD, CLOCK_BAR), distils records into story events through the pure module, folds them, and on each bar asks the selector for a beat. Records republished by a replay (`cause: 'replay'`) and folds during replay playback are skipped — a replayed take never counts its history twice (proven by test). Beat rolls come from the existing seeded path (`hashU32` over the project seed), so the same recorded inputs fold to the same book and the same beats (proven by test).
- **The bundle decision:** the new storage key was caught by `bundleKeys.selfcheck` and given its decision in `src/state/bundle.js` — LEFT OUT of project bundles, reason recorded, matching the engagement scores' precedent ("earned here, never carried in a project").
- **Where it surfaces:** backstage only — the dev Mind panel gains a Story section (session, phase, intensity, beats used, the two standings with each persona's believed read beside the truth, open grudges, the recent beat log), fed by `getStoryView()` / `subscribeStory()`. The view deliberately carries **no suspicion fields at all** — no value, no stage, no anomaly count (see the tension flagged below). THE ROOM panel is untouched.

## Event → standing weights (as implemented)

Every event moves **mood by the full weight** and **standing by α = 0.1 of it**. Mood decays ×0.995 per bar (half-life ≈ 138 bars) and resets each session; standing never decays — it is the relationship's memory.

| event (from the real bus) | esteem (LOIS→Davis) | trust (Davis→LOIS) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LOIS verdict (critique), delta d (contract caps \|d\| ≤ 0.25) | d × 48 (max ±12) | d × 48 (the same exchange, read from his side) |
| LOIS burn verdict (BURN state) | −14 flat | −14 flat |
| dice crown (DICE_CAST, his crowns) | +4 | +3 |
| bloom (BLOOM, a garden moment) | +6 | +5 |
| artist keep (KEEP_ADD) | +3 | +6 (her judgment vindicated weighs more on his side) |
| Davis proposal (his exposure) | — | −1 (small, constant) |

- **Grudges:** a verdict at or beyond the contract's own honesty band (delta ≤ −0.1) is an *offence* on its topic; praise (delta ≥ +0.1) retires offences one-for-one. The **third offence on a topic promotes** the record into a named grudge pattern (research §1.6.4); three praises close a record entirely — forgiveness is the record closing, never a speech. A burn is an offence on the topic 'show'.
- **Belief ≠ truth (§1.6.5):** a persona's believed standings refresh **only when that persona acts** (proposes, verdicts, crowns, blooms, keeps, leans). Between their own acts their read goes stale, and the gap is measured at the moment of acting: a gap ≥ 12 becomes the episode's *misread* pattern, which the other persona may later name. The anomaly-evidence split (below) is the hard rule and is not interpreted — it is arithmetic.

## Beats and episodes (as built)

- **Phases:** an episode opens in **callback** when the session-start sift found ledger patterns (a keep that was once passed = a *debt*; a palette kept three sessions running = a *streak*; the previous session's last keep = the *last ending*), otherwise in **working**. Sifted patterns and rising intensity (≥ 0.55) move it to **build**; a peak-class beat fires the single **peak**; intensity under 0.15 drops to **release**; the valley (< 0.2 in build) returns to **working**. Intensity is fed by events (verdict .25 · crown .2 · bloom .35 · keep .15 · proposal .1 · route .05 · room shift .15 · grudge promotion .5) and decays ×0.99 per bar.
- **Peak quota: one per session**, enforced in the selector — a second promotion in one session is sifted into the book but can never fire as a peak.
- **Salience order** (research rule 2 — the queue is ordered, never shuffled): grudge promotion → suspicion milestone → callback → grudge → streak → reversal → misread → truce → bed note → **silence**. A candidate that fails its gates falls through to the next; nothing fires twice to fill a gap.
- **The eight anti-repetition rules, by construction:** (1) forms are drawn per beat-kind from a **shuffle-bag that persists in the book** — a form is consumed, not recycled, until the bag empties; (2) salience beats variety (above); (3) the cleverest forms — the dry note, the too-quick milestone — are **gated to ~10% of rolls**; (4) cooldowns at four levels: topic (96 bars), bed note (8 bars), move (once per phase instance), storylet (a grudge fires at most once per session); (5) beats are **composed from ledger referents** (the actual topic, count, standing gap) — nothing is a free-floating quip; (6) functional bed lines are exempt from the story budget and can never be gated into silence — if the bag's clever form is holstered, the note falls back to plain; (7) there are no generated lines in this slice, so the gates here *are* the gates any future line source must pass — the selector is the only door; (8) **silence is a valid beat**: an empty candidate list returns no beat, and the room stays quiet.
- **Session story budget: 12 story beats** (flagged default, Q7); bed notes don't count against it.

## The suspicion arc: exact arithmetic

- **Anomalies — only two kinds, both verified against the fold, never inferred:**
  - **flip** — a verdict whose sign reverses the previous verdict *on the same topic* with **no work event folded between them** (no proposal, crown, bloom or keep since). If work intervened, the flip is an honest reversal pattern, not an anomaly. Witness: Davis (a critique is LOIS's act; he is the one positioned to notice it move).
  - **lean** — the third persona's lean signature (which keys are leaned) changes while the mood she reported is unchanged since the last lean. The first lean ever seen establishes the baseline and is never an anomaly. Witness: whichever persona acted most recently (they were in the room), else Davis.
  - A third candidate from the research — room/route shifts with no persona cause — was **rejected as unverifiable**: dice rolls are not bus records, so a shift cannot be proven uncaused, and charging it would charge the *artist's own rolls* to her. Flagged here rather than built.
- **Increments:** an anomaly counts only if **≥ 3 sessions** have passed since the last counted one. The step is **Δ = 6 × (85 − s) / 85** — asymptotic: the value approaches **85 and can never reach it**, let alone pass it (proven by test over 60 rationed increments). Anomalies inside the gap are recorded as counts, never as movement.
- **Milestones 20 / 40 / 60 / 80:** crossing one arms it in the book (it persists across sessions until it can fire). It fires as a peak-class beat only at **engagement tier ≥ 3** (the research's mid tier) for either persona, only in the build phase, and it **loses the peak slot to a genuine peak** (a grudge promotion the same session spends the quota first; the milestone stays armed). Forms by milestone: 20 a **joke**, 40 a **citation**, 60 a **half-theory**, 80 **too-quick** (also the clever-gated form). Firing is the mundane exit: the beat happens, and the value **talks down by 5, floored at the previous milestone** — stage regression by ordinary talk, exactly as §02 requires. The arc **never resolves** and is **never named**: no stage name, milestone or value appears in any view, including the backstage one (test-proven: the serialized view contains neither the arc's name nor the third persona's).
- **Evidence split:** each anomaly kind has exactly one witness persona (above); `evidenceOf` returns only that persona's anomalies. Neither persona ever holds the whole picture.
- **Artist-proofing:** increments are rationed by sessions and require bus-verifiable contradictions; an artist rolling dice or shifting the room produces no anomalies at all (rolls aren't records; route shifts aren't anomalies), so the arc cannot be farmed deliberately.

- **Q7 (beat budget):** 12 story beats per session; bed exempt.
- **Q8 (episode catalogue):** the single five-phase shape, content driven by sifted patterns rather than a catalogue of authored episodes (the research's "one generic walk first" option).
- Smaller calls, same treatment: truce arms at standing ≥ 25 (Q1-adjacent); the misread threshold is 12 points of standing; belief staleness uses the "refresh when the persona acts" reading of §1.6.5 (the evidence split is the hard rule and is exact).

## Honest gaps and tensions (read these)

- **The beats fire backstage; nothing downstream consumes them yet.** This is deliberate and matches the standing design: the rules backend speaks no lines "until Matt designs what they may do", and the mind's decision-context shape is pinned by selfcheck — widening it so a persona's *decisions* could lean on story state is a decision-shape change this slice was forbidden to make. What S3 publishes is the machine, its persistence, its backstage view, and the beat log; the seam for a future consumer is `getStoryView()` / `subscribeStory()`. When a backend is allowed story context, it plugs in there.
- **Suspicion vs "backstage/dev only":** the brief says story state surfaces backstage; the research (§1.8.7) says the arc's meter "may not be displayed, *including in debug UI*". The research wins for the arc specifically: standings, grudges and episodes are backstage; the arc is nowhere. Its beats, when they fire, appear in the backstage beat log by kind only.
- **Sheds are not story events yet** — for the same reason S1 flagged: the bus carries no shed record today (the governor's shed log doesn't enumerate its cuts). Director-room shifts contribute episode intensity (0.15) and nothing else. If a shed record ever lands on the bus, it distils like the others.
- **One design fix found by testing:** the episode's valley rule originally required "no fresh pattern waiting", which let a pattern stuck behind a cooldown pin the episode in build forever (and with it, every move-level cooldown). The valley now reads intensity alone; a waiting pattern is pressure, not a lock. Covered in `storyState.selfcheck`.
