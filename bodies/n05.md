## Context

HYPE's `BitmapCanvas` is a persistent bitmap painted every frame and never
cleared; shapes build up over time. It is where much of the density and
painterliness in Davis's work comes from, and it is free long-exposure motion
blur.

Kinetic Curator structurally cannot do this today: React re-renders the SVG
declaratively, so each frame replaces the last.

## Conflict with the render pipeline

Accumulated history would live only in the pixel buffer, not the SVG DOM.
Pause and export at 4× and the built-up history is gone — you get the
current frame's shapes, sharp and alone. Resolving it means either:

- accumulating at export resolution continuously (a persistent 7680×4320 RGBA buffer — 132 MB), or
- replaying the last N frames at high res on export (which reintroduces a deterministic-time refactor this spec otherwise avoids).

## Status

Defer — own project. Wait until blend modes and gradient shading are in and
their effect on the look is known before deciding whether accumulation is
still worth the structural cost.
