# Greiman — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

Her methods are the four plays the spec rules for her (Q13):

- **Bitmap type** — baked coarse-pixel glyph variants: the letter as a digital artifact, not a drawing.
- **Layered planes** — type and shapes in translucent depth layers.
- **The digital spectrum** — gradient colour run across a word or block.
- **The float** — the `greiman` mode as it stands today: diagonal step, snap, echo.

She also owns the PATTERN GLYPH mode and the PATTERN TIDES mode (spec §4).
