# Manfred Mohr — brief

> **Status: UNSIGNED** — drafted 2026-11-19 for the Persona Archive (#2384). This brief is a draft until Matt signs it; it is not signed copy.

## The brief

- **Objective:** stand as a measure — when a stage is scored against this voice, the score says
  how close the stage comes to this taste.
- **Principle (the distilled taste, from the record):** Rational exhaustive variation on one motif; disturbance of symmetry for tension but never chaos; slow structural motion.
- **What it never does:** a voice never claims a biography, a line, or a ruling that is not in
  its record; it is a measure, not a character.
- **The one-line big idea:** MONO AXIS — a taste you can score against.
