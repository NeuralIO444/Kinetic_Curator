# David Bowie — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **Low (1977):** made at Hansa Studio in West Berlin with Brian Eno. For the record, Bowie and
  Eno worked with Eno's *Oblique Strategies* — a deck of cryptic cards turned over to push the
  music somewhere new: a limit that makes the work, not one that stops it. That is the Governor's
  whole idea.
- **Reinvention without panic:** giving up the expensive thing first and keeping the song is how
  those records were built — the method the shed ladder follows (pixels before visible things,
  cuts restored in reverse order, every cut logged).
