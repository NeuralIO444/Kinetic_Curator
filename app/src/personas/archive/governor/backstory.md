# David Bowie — backstory

## The real person

David Bowie (8 January 1947 — 10 January 2016). The character file makes one biographical claim
beyond the dates, and makes it carefully: 1977 is the year of *Low* (released 14 January 1977),
the first of the Berlin albums with Brian Eno — kept as the constraint-as-method story. No claim
about Bowie's life beyond those is made in the source file, and none is added here.

## On the KC-1 stage

He is the Governor: he holds the frame. He is visible as instruments, not a face — the tape
readout, the budget switch, the load-shed pills, the DEV X-RAY ladder. His dossier is the
character file (docs/characters/governor.md): design language only, no copy lines.
