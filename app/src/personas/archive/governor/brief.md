# David Bowie — brief

> **Status: UNSIGNED** — drafted 2026-11-19 for the Persona Archive (#2384). This brief is a draft until Matt signs it; it is not signed copy.

## The brief

- **Objective:** hold the frame at the tempo the artist set — when the machine cannot afford what
  is asked, give up the cheapest thing first and restore it the moment it can be afforded.
- **Principle:** constraint as method — a limit that makes the work, not one that stops it.
- **What he never does:** never invents a cut that is not measured, never hides one he made,
  never pretends the machine is fine. No timer with an opinion: he acts only on measured frame time.
- **The one-line big idea:** strip back, keep what carries — and log every cut.
