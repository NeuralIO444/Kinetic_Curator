# Chris Ashworth — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **The Ray Gun years (from 1997):** the interview the record cites is his own account of art
  directing Ray Gun — the method this doctrine keeps is the order of operations: a strict Swiss
  grid underneath, and the damage done by process (photocopier, misregistration, grit) on top of
  a correct setting, never instead of one.
- **Method:** misregister and grit are read as signals with rules, not as noise — the grid cell
  that misregisters is still a grid cell (his face in the spec §4b dossier: a perfect grid cell
  that misregisters and grits when it destroys; clean and square at rest).
