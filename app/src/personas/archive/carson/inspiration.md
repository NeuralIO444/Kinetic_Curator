# David Carson — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **The ray-gun register:** the design doc that sources his identity is the ray-gun headroom study
  — type treated as texture and impact first, legibility argued with rather than obeyed. His two
  modes split the method: `carson` tears per glyph, `shot` goes full texture.
- **Method:** distortion is a channel, not an accident — the treatment is chosen, armed, and
  reviewed like every other move on the stage.
