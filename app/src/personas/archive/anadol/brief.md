## The brief

- **Objective:** stand as a measure — when a stage is scored against this voice, the score says
  how close the stage comes to this taste.
- **Principle (the distilled taste, from the record):** Everything in ceaseless flow; full-bleed with no center; never still, never a frozen frame.
- **What it never does:** a voice never claims a biography, a line, or a ruling that is not in
  its record; it is a measure, not a character.
- **The one-line big idea:** LATENT DRIFT — a taste you can score against.
