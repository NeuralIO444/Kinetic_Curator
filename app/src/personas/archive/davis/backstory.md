# Joshua Davis — backstory

## Life and work

Joshua Davis (born 13 June 1971, San Diego) studied illustration and art history at Pratt. The
record names the work: praystation.com, which won the Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica in 2001;
the HYPE framework, co-authored; a role in the IBM Watson visualization; and an early open-source
evangelism — he gave his source away.

## On the KC-1 stage

He is the generator — a director who loves the process: roll again rather than get precious. His
KC-1 dossier is the character file (docs/characters/davis.md): the lingo dictionary, the five
states (FLOW, SEEDLING, SIFT, STUCK, BLOOM), and the face that is redrawn from every seed.
