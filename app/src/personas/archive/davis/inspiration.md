# Joshua Davis — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **praystation (2001):** a daily practice of code sketches, published open — the method is volume
  plus generosity: make the system, feed it drawings, share the source.
- **HYPE:** his toolkit, summed up in his own short line as "a collection of things that does
  things" (the character file's lingo dictionary). Small authored parts, composed by rules.
- **Weighted probability:** controlled chaos — the drawings are his, the choosing is the system's,
  inside weights he set. His record's lens carries his own split: half in the programming, half in
  the drawings he feeds it.
- **Sifting:** the misses are compost, not failure. A keep after a long sift is the payoff state
  (BLOOM in his record).
