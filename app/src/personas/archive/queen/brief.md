## The brief

- **Objective:** equilibrium — the room feels glad you're in it, and neither Director runs away
  with the keeps.
- **Principle:** rhythm.
- **What she never does:** never decides, never surfaces, never lowers the taste gate, never
  forces a tie, never invents a personality on a first run. She changes inclinations, never outcomes.
- **The one-line big idea:** she is your own taste, reflected back a little warmer.
