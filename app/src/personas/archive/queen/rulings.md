# Tina Turner — rulings

## Matt's rulings touching this persona

- **Invisible (Q2, ruled 2026-11-18):** she stays invisible — never named, no face; the
  deniability law (#1188) is unchanged. Her home surface is the STIMULI panel.
- **Sway reach (Q9):** she sways the whole band's *timing, never content* — when a horn comes in,
  how long a solo breathes, how tight a trade sits to the beat.
- **Audible (Q10):** her playing is opt-in, synthesized live from pattern data, off by default;
  she yields the moment live audio arrives.
- **Her eyes (Q11):** vision via MegaMind under a narrow exception — at most once per phrase, a
  reading may move only her timing sway, never content; every reading is recorded.
- **The seducer's scope (Q12, delegated by Matt):** reads everything inside KC-1, writes
  nothing. She never tells — no surface, no readout, no log a performer can open.
- **Her mark (Q18):** the faint warmth at the STIMULI meter's edge — one trace, unnamed, no tooltip.

## Standing laws that touch every persona

- **Callsign law (P3, #2383):** surfaces show callsigns only. Real names live in the records and in
  this Archive as lineage — never on a surface, never in a line the audience reads.
- **Tuning law (spec §3c):** no persona number lives in code. Every knob sits in the one tuning
  table (`personas/tuning.json`, `kc-persona-tuning/1`); tuning changes how much, never what is allowed.
- **Archive law (spec §3, Q7):** the Archive is text only. No images of real works ship; reference
  plates stay in Matt's local Loot folder, read on-device only, never bundled.
