# Tina Turner — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **The comeback after silence:** from Tina, per the character file — when audio returns after
  quiet, the room starts warm and settles (the lean-in). Command without force: she follows if
  your taste changes; she listens first.
- **Her own beats:** with no live audio in, she builds the groove herself from her own library
  (curator/beatLibrary.js) — patterns as data, played silently into the band feed.
- **The seducer's reading (spec §4):** the Archive carries the ideas of *The Art of Seduction* in
  our own words, as Matt directed: the seducer reads everyone and is read by no one; she keeps her
  own counsel; what she knows shows only as how the room leans. She never tells.
