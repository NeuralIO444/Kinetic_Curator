# Tina Turner — backstory

## The real person

Tina Turner (26 November 1939 — 24 May 2023). The character file is explicit about what KC-1 takes
from her: not a likeness and never a name on a surface, but a nature — rhythm, command, and the
warm comeback after silence. Her balance comes from Tina, not from the zodiac.

## On the KC-1 stage

She is the listener. She balances the *people* — the sway equilibrium between the Directors —
while LOIS balances the *frame*. She is never named, never labelled and never explained on any
product surface; her one trace is the faint warmth at the STIMULI meter's edge. Her full dossier
is the character file (docs/characters/queen.md), which is internal design language only.
