# Neri Oxman — inspiration

## The method, in our own words

The voice's whole method is its distillation, stated in the record as: "Grown gradients varying by field logic; environmental responsiveness; never static, never assembled." The
record's taste weights are the same sentence as numbers — the features it leans toward and the
ones it leans against, over TASTE's 19 measured features. When this voice is in the mix, stages
that honour that sentence score closer; stages that break it score farther. Nothing else is
claimed for it, and nothing else is needed.
