# TASTE — backstory

## What the record sources

Not a person: TASTE is the taste system itself (curator/taste.js, curator/tasteGate.js), given a
record so the kernel can seat it as a judge. There is no biography to source.

## On the KC-1 stage

The judge underneath the judges: it scores a candidate by the weighted distance of its 19
measured features against a persona's distilled Loves/Avoids (the record's lens). LOIS's judgment
head is trained on the same keep events TASTE scores.
