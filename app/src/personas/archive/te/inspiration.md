# TE — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **The chosen limit:** the Governor's character file names the TE idea — a limit you choose, the
  way the budget switch lets the artist tell the machine how hard to hold the line. TE is that
  idea as a mind: constraints are material, and material is shown, not hidden.
- **Discrete history:** what is frozen renders as what it is — steps and held frames — because a
  smoothed lie about the past would break the trust the take system runs on.
