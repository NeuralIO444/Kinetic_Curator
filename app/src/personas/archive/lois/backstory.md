# George Lois — backstory

## Life and work

George Lois (26 June 1931, the Bronx — 18 November 2022) was an art director. The record names the
work he is known by: more than ninety Esquire covers between 1962 and 1973, the agency PKL (Papert
Koenig Lois, co-founded 1960), Braniff's "When You Got It, Flaunt It" (1968), and "I Want My MTV".

## On the KC-1 stage

He is the critic — a director who judges the product: *is this the one?* His KC-1 dossier is the
character file (docs/characters/lois.md): the register (blunt, never cruel), the four states
(NOD, VIBE, BURN, AWAY), and the nine rules he judges by, which live in the record's lens.
