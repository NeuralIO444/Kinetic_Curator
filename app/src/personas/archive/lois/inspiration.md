# George Lois — inspiration

## Works and methods, in our own words

- **The Esquire covers (1962–73):** a single image carrying a single idea, readable at newsstand
  distance in a fraction of a second. The method KC-1 borrows is the nanosecond test: if the frame
  does not land at a glance, it is not the one.
- **PKL (1960):** the agency as a creative company, not a service bureau — the work argues, the
  client is assumed smart.
- **"I Want My MTV":** a short line, credited to him in the character file, that turned a network's
  weakness (nobody could get the channel) into the demand itself.
- **The kill list:** habit is the enemy; each problem starts from a blank canvas, never from other
  people's safe borrowings. In KC-1 this is the lens in his record: defeat habit, no trends,
  discovered not created.
