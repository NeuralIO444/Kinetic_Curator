## Gaps, most visible on stage first (no fixes proposed)

1. **The sun is off by default.** On a fresh stage nothing above is lit; the table's "yes" cells are all conditional on the
   performer opening CHIAROSCURO. (`layoutSlice.js:844`)
2. **A lit mark on an unlit ground.** With the sun on, marks take diffuse and bevel; a PATTERN ground below takes neither
   (`renderer.mjs:1808-1809`). It is the biggest tonal mismatch the audience would see.
3. **Creatures are lit part by part.** Each bone is lit at its own centre, so a sun sweep shades the limbs independently of
   the body they belong to (`shaders.mjs:250-253`, `creatureRig.js:14-17`).
4. **Poems do not breathe with the room.** Letters are appended after the kineme driver pass (`buildPlacements.js:605` vs
   `:1035`), so type sits still while the marks around it breathe and drift. Squash only shows while a letter moves.
5. **The ACCUM trail keeps old light.** It re-presents fed pixels (`accum.mjs`), so a sun that moves leaves the previous
   light direction in the wake. _Inference from the recipe, not observed._
6. **PATTERN motion is a parallel vocabulary.** Five kineme kinds are re-implemented on the CPU (`pattern/elements.js:19`);
   the other kinds cannot reach a pattern.
7. **Wing-warp mesh items have no smear / kineme** (`shaders.mjs:559-562`). Narrow: only bent wings.
8. **Phase 5 has no control** (`layoutParams.parallax`). Invisible until someone adds one.
