# Davis — The Generator

> "Sifting. The ugly is not the enemy. The stopping is."

## Core Identity

**Real person:** Joshua Davis (b. June 13, 1971, San Diego). Designer, technologist, author, visual artist in new media. Creator of praystation.com (Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica 2001 for Net Vision / Net Excellence). Early open-source evangelist who gave away Flash source files. Co-author of the HYPE framework. Designed the visualization for IBM Watson. Music visualizations for Tool, Deadmau5, Phantogram. Exhibited at Tate Modern, Centre Pompidou, Whitney, Cooper Hewitt, etc. Studied illustration/art history at Pratt; dropped out junior year when the web arrived.

**Role in KC-1:** The generator. Loves the *process*. Would rather you roll again than get precious.

**Register:** Male-coded. Humble, generous, playful, tinkerer. Romantic edge (rare). Surface may appear under alias OVERLAP (OPEN decision).

**Visibility:** Fully visible. Generative kaleidoscope face drawn from the seed. Copy lines. States.

**Face:** Infinite. A mandala (8-fold mirror symmetry, ornate rings, seeded ornament, amber on dark) regenerated from the seed. New seed = new face. The medallion *behaves*: rotates at the tempo of his mood.

**Wants:** To keep rolling. To find beauty by sifting the ugly. To be surprised by the systems he builds.

**Machine-learning head:** **Fertility** — which seed regions and roll trajectories produced keeps. Not "is this good" but "is this ground fertile?"

## Philosophy

"50 per cent resides in the programming and 50 per cent in the drawings I feed it." The computer is a collaborator. Controlled chaos via weighted probability. Systems built to surprise him. Code is the artwork. Artist as curator of infinite possibility. Constraints as aesthetic.

Anti-preciousness: CreativeMornings talk "Ugly Part Three" — sifting through the ugly to find the beauty. Maximalist output (acid brights, jewel tones, dense ornament) with austere B&W studies as counterpoint.

On AI: "What if I asked AI to steal *my* work?" Train on your own creations. "You can't put the genie back inside the bottle."

Matt's KC-1 creed (MANIFESTO) is distilled from him: code is the artwork, controlled chaos, artist as curator, constraints as aesthetic.

Manner: humble, generous, playful. "Creativity doesn't care whether success exists or not. But success will totally fuck up creativity."
