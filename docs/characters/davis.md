## Where his philosophy comes from
"50 per cent resides in the programming and 50 per cent in the drawings I
feed it": the computer is a collaborator. Controlled chaos by weighted
probability; systems built to surprise him. His talk "Ugly Part Three" is the
root of the sifting state (shown as SIFT; he rarely says the word himself). Matt's KC-1 creed (the manifesto) is distilled from
him: code is the artwork, controlled chaos, artist as curator, constraints as
aesthetic. On the surface his voice may appear under an alias (OVERLAP);
DAVIS vs alias is still **OPEN**.

## His five states (all process, all honest)
| State (on screen) | Means | Real signal |
|---|---|---|
| FLOW | Rolling at a good clip | Roll rate |
| SEEDLING | Fresh seed, curiosity | New seed dropped |
| **SIFT** (internal code `UGLY`) | Sifting. Not failure | Heavy passing |
| STUCK | Nudges you to roll | Revisiting one seed, no keeps |
| BLOOM | The payoff | A keep after a long sift |

## How his face behaves
The medallion rotates at the tempo of his mood: slow in FLOW, agitated in
SIFT, barely turning in STUCK, quickening in SEEDLING. You read his state
across the room without reading a word. Continuous signal, so he renders
as Davis (design-system rule 2).

## Relationships
- **You:** your internal process partner. He wants you to keep sifting.
- **LOIS:** calls him "George" (respect: the pointing is usually right).
  "He found his one in my compost pile. You're welcome, George."
- **The Queen:** he never knows. She widens his rolling range when the room
  is audio-rich and settles it when quiet, and her warmth can bring out his
  rare romantic line ("She likes tonight."). Davis is the romantic; LOIS
  would never.

## What he never does
- Never a timer with an opinion; every state is a measured signal.
- Never acts on his own: the verdict strip names the room, it doesn't act.
- Never precious, and never claims sole authorship of the surprise: the
  system is the collaborator. Stopping is the enemy, not the misses.
