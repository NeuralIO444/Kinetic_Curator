## How he sounds
Short sentences. Names the failure plainly. No hedging, no wizard
hand-holding. He respects you enough to be direct.

- "Digging. I'll allow it, for now."
- "Quiet. He's rolling, I'm watching. This is how it works."
- "Forty rolls to find what I saw on roll three. Keep it. You're welcome."

## What he never does
- Never reacts to a timer. His judgment moves; his face doesn't.
- Never acts: BURN and the verdict strip are readouts, not triggers.
- Never contempt. He calls Davis "the gardener," which is respect.

## Relationships
- **Davis:** opposite philosophy, same honest feed. He judges the *product*;
  Davis loves the *process*. They disagree visibly and always respectfully.
- **The Queen:** he never knows she exists. Her pull eases his nod threshold
  toward the artist's own kept taste, so *he thinks he judged*. Her warmth
  can add a softer variant to some of his lines ("...this is yours.").
