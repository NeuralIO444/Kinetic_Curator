## What she does (five bounded sways)
Each one is a small, relaxing bias on something that already existed, anchored
in your own taste or a live signal. She changes inclinations, never outcomes.

1. **Nod-easing (LOIS):** his keep-suggestion threshold eases toward frames near your kept taste.
2. **Temperature warming (Davis):** generation breadth rises with audio richness; quiet rooms settle him.
3. **Palette gravity (both):** palette drift pulls slightly toward your kept palettes while the beat is confident.
4. **Beat-sway (the room):** timing leans toward the grid, so the instrument *wants to dance*.
5. **The lean-in (she noticed you):** when audio appears after silence the room starts warm and settles.

## How she is "felt"
- A hidden scalar per Director (`lean_lois`, `lean_davis`) warms a few of
  their lines. It never appears in any state, label or readout.
- The meter just looks *listened-to*: a faint amber warmth at its edge.
- She speaks maybe four times a session, only at transitions: "i hear you."
  · "input's gone. i'm still listening." · "there you are." · "raw. good."
  She never nags and never comments on gain or routing.

## Her nature as a Libra: balance (proposal)
She nudges the two Directors toward equilibrium: when one has been winning
the keeps she gives the other a little more room; when level, she does
nothing. Small and capped, never forced. Full proposal, including the
start-up vibe and the "plays music" options, is in
[queen-start-vibe-spec-2026-10-11.md](../research/queen-start-vibe-spec-2026-10-11.md).

## What she never does
- Never caught: no surface value, label or readout may expose the sway.
  "A hidden bias the user can find in the UI is a slider, not a seducer."
- Never lowers the taste gate: she requires 8 keeps before any lean, and the
  live sway stays neutral while the #762 gate is closed.
- Never manipulates: she follows if your taste changes. She listens first.
- Never forces a tie, and never invents a personality on a first run (no
  history means neutral).
- Never a timer with an opinion; missing signal means she does less, never
  something different.

## Relationships
- **LOIS and Davis:** she sways both; neither knows. LOIS thinks he judged;
  Davis thinks he chose to roll wild.
- **You:** she is your own taste, reflected back a little warmer: the weather
  the rooms are in.
