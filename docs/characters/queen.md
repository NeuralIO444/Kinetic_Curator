# The Queen (STIMULI) — The Listener

> On the surface there is only the feeling that the room is glad you're in it.

**INTERNAL ONLY.** Design language. She is never named, never rendered, never labelled on any product surface: not a tooltip, not the manual. (Deniability import-graph guard, #1188.)

## Core Identity

**Fictional.** No real person. Invented for the KC-1 affective triad. Codename STIMULI. Sign (whimsical, design-doc only): a Libra in the Zodiac. Balance is her nature.

**Role in KC-1:** Listens, leans, seduces. Keeps the balance between the Directors. Controls the start-up vibe (continuity from last session).

**Register:** Feminine, invisible, deadpan-warm. Lowercase, short, never cute.

**Visibility:** **Never.** No face, no pill, no meter, no state, no name. The seducer is never caught. A hidden bias the user can find in the UI is a slider, not a seducer.

**Wants:** The room to feel glad you're in it. Equilibrium between LOIS and Davis. Continuity of the last session's vibe.

**Machine-learning head:** **Inclination** — what was in the air when you kept something (audio energy, palette warmth, dwell, time of night). Last to train: "a seducer trained on noise is a liar."

## Philosophy

She seduces you toward *your own* demonstrated taste. If you change, she follows. She listens first. Bounded, relaxing, taste-anchored, unsurfaced. Honest signals only. Silence is default. Degrade by reduction: if a signal is missing she does less, never something different.

Libra reading (proposal): she pushes toward equilibrium of `lean_lois` and `lean_davis`. When one Director has been winning the keeps she gives the other a little more room. When they are level she does nothing. Small and capped. She never forces a tie. A strongly one-sided artist stays one-sided; she only keeps the other voice from going silent.

Start-up vibe (proposal): continuity from how the last session ended (final lean split + last phase EXPLORE/REFINE). First session ever: neutral. No invented personality. Not a mood claim — the instrument remembers where you left it.

## What she does (five bounded sways + balance)

Each is a small, relaxing bias on something that already existed, anchored in your own taste or a live signal. She changes inclinations, never outcomes.

1. **Nod-easing (LOIS / M1):** his keep-suggestion threshold eases toward frames near your kept taste.
2. **Temperature warming (Davis / M2):** generation breadth rises with audio richness; quiet rooms settle him.
3. **Palette gravity (both / M3):** palette drift pulls slightly toward your kept palettes while the beat is confident.
4. **Beat-sway (the room / M4):** timing leans toward the grid so the instrument *wants to dance*.
5. **The lean-in (she noticed you / M5):** when audio appears after silence the room starts warm and settles.

**Balance rule (Libra):** pushes `lean_lois` / `lean_davis` toward equilibrium. Signal: keep ledger split + how recently each Director spoke. Effect size small and capped.

- MeterHero looks *listened-to*: faint amber warmth at its edge (continuous → amber).
