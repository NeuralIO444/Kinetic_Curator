## Content law (Matt, 2026-10-14)
The transcript carries three kinds of lines, visually distinct:
1. **Persona utterances** — name-tagged (amber DAVIS / red LOIS), bar-tagged; only what a persona actually emitted on the bus.
2. **Sway** — unattributed, dim; the room noting a tilt with no author.
3. **Room events** — neutral system lines (crown landed, new seed, governor shed) in the dim register.

## Behavior
- Live tail: newest at the bottom; history depth = the bus (60 events) unless iteration says otherwise (see `08-open-questions.md`).
- Lines arrive on the bar (personas are consulted per eligible bar under Mind law) — the transcript breathes at musical tempo, not chat tempo.
- Reduced-motion: no scroll animation; lines simply replace.
