# 02 — The Queen (in the Room)

Status: her law LOCKED (Mind doctrine); the suspicion arc is NEW design, approved in direction 2026-10-14.

## Her law here (as everywhere)
- No window. No face. No name. No transcript lines of her own.
- She appears only as **sway**: unattributed room events in the transcript ("the room tilts") and as effects on the other two (a tilt in a fill, a tempo nudge in Stimuli).
- taste.json is read-only to every model; she remains the artist's kept taste, not a character anyone can address.

## The suspicion arc (the long game)
Premise: Davis and LOIS slowly come to suspect *something* else is in the room.
- **Fuel:** real sway events. When sway fires, there is a small chance (tier-gated) one of them notices — a beat, not a popup.
- **Shape:** stages of suspicion — dismissal → noticing patterns → pet theories (always wrong, always in character) → quiet vigilance. Never confirmation. Never her name.
- **Pacing:** rare early, deepens with engagement tier and across sessions; arc flags persist. A drip measured in weeks is the point.
- **The meter (research-derived):** suspicion is a scalar that rises **only on ledger-verifiable sway anomalies** (never on a timer, never causable by anything the artist does deliberately), and is **capped asymptotically at ~85** — certainty is unreachable by construction. Milestone beats at 20/40/60/80, each shipped with a mundane exit that lets the persona talk themselves back down. Minimum 3 sessions between increments. **Evidence is split** (Bad News): Davis has seen some tilts, LOIS others; their theories disagree because their evidence genuinely differs.
- **Lines:** hand-banked at Mind level; model-written within beats at MegaMind level (see `07-megamind-tier.md` — where they start *testing* for her).

## Guardrails
- Suspicion never becomes accusation of the artist, never breaks a performance, never surfaces in any UI outside the transcript.
- If the arc ever feels close to resolving, it resets a stage — the mystery is load-bearing.
