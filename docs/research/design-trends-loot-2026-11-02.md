## KC-1 mapping (coordinator's read, not a ruling)

Where a label touches systems KC-1 already has, noted for a future Look pass:

- **Risograph** — print grain, misregistration, limited-ink texture. Already
  in KC-1's blood (the wet/print lanes); the closest thing here to a native
  Look family.
- **Exaggerated Type** — oversized lettering as the composition. FIELD's
  baked-stamp poems are a more disciplined cousin; the loot version would push
  scale until type is the stage, not an element on it.
- **Cybercore** — Y2K chrome, wireframe, tech-specimen graphics. Doubled
  across both lists, and the same wave as the Brain Dead Cybercon programming
  Matt flagged the same night; a chrome/specimen Look is unclaimed territory
  in the current deck.
- **Digital Decay** — glitch, degradation, signal loss as the aesthetic.
  KC-1 owns this mechanism already (the glitch corruption keyed to the
  Tighten band); as a Look it would mean decay as the resting state, not the
  exception.
- **Anthropomorphic** — creatures with faces and feelings. The WetLab
  direction (creatures as assembled citizens, "no longer a sticker"); a Look
  in this family would push expression/readability of the creature body.
- **Tactile Craft / Claymorphism** — hand-made and soft-3D materiality. The
  layered-marks creature target ("very layered") is the tactile end; clay is
  the rendered-material end, nearer BlockWalk's Blender world than the stage.
- **Low-Poly** — faceted low-count geometry. Native to BlockWalk; on the
  KC-1 stage it reads as a formation/geometry accent rather than a Look.

Unmapped, recorded for completeness: Acid Brutalism, Digital Fantasy,
Illustrated Bento, Kawaii, Playful Pop, Neo Minimalism, Mid Century, Vector
Heart, iOS Core, Ethereal, New Maximalism. (Ethereal and New Maximalism are
near-opposites of each other; the deck already has quiet and maximal rooms
of its own.)
