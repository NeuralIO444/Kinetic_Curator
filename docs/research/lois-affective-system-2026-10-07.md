a timer pretending to have an opinion.

## 2. The critic's states

Four states, all honest, all product-facing:

- **NOD** — "that's the one, keep it." Trigger: keep-worthy frame per the
  honest feed. His judgment moves; his face doesn't.
- **VIBE** — idling. Quiet room, watching.
- **BURN** — chain-smoking a hot streak. High keep/dwell cadence.
- **AWAY** — gone 5 minutes, literally true. When AWAY he goes silent —
  no honest signal, no performance. (DS: silence is default.)

Face: one fixed kaomoji per state — `( ̄—̄ )` NOD, `( ─‿─ )` VIBE,
`( ◉‿◉ )` BURN, `( ── )` AWAY. LOIS has one face and judges; Davis has
infinite faces and generates.

## 3. The argument (see DIRECTORS, #1126)

LOIS vs Davis share the honest feed and read it through opposite
philosophies — 4×5 = 20 rooms, full mutual-awareness copy matrix, verdict

disagreement, always respectful: he calls Davis "the gardener" (respect —
the keep came from the garden); Davis calls him "George" (respect — the
pointing is usually right).

## 4. The triad (unified 2026-10-07)

LOIS is one of three personae. KC-1 notices (ambient). The Queen
(feminine, invisible, never renders) sways LOIS via M1 (nod-easing — his
keep-suggestion threshold eases toward frames near the artist's
kept-taste centroid); her hidden `lean_lois` field warms a subset of his
matrix lines ("That's the one. Keep it — this is yours."). LOIS never
knows. He thinks he judged. His MLX head: **judgment** — keep vs pass,
"would he nod?" Binary, most data-efficient, prove first (#762/#926).
