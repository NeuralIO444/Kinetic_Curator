# Kinetic Curator — instrument creed

People should be able to say *that looks like Kinetic Curator* the way they say *that looks like a Davis plate* or *that looks like a Haeckel lithograph*. The look is not a filter. It is a set of refusals.

## What this is

A **slick realtime instrument** for composing living stills. Not a film renderer that happens to preview. Not a social toy. Not a soup of particles with extra sliders.

The operator plays a 1000×700 stage the way a printmaker plays a plate: few good specimens, honest materials, a clock you can feel.

## Pillars

1. **Realtime is the product.** If it cannot hold 60 Hz at the advertised quality cap, cut nodes or move the stage offline. Do not fake smoothness with motion CSS on a live swarm.
2. **Bake heavy, evaluate cheap.** Geometry morphs, CLIP, diffusion, Python, MLX — studio only. The tab sets a scalar `u` and stamps `<use>`. No path interpolation on rAF. No model on rAF.
3. **Honesty.** A control that does not drive the kernel is disabled or labeled `(soon)`. A blend the farm cannot honor warns once. Versions on screen match `package.json`.
4. **Canon is sacred; overlay is play.** The shipped 137 never receive session junk. User traces (Haeckel plates, moth wings) live on the project as `user:*`.
5. **Sleeper UI.** One enum beats six sliders. Species radii stay on the `dyn` seed channel. The artist sees BEHAVE, not SoA.
6. **Two layers of drawing.** Base = geometry (path, compound, ladder frame). Material = fill (gradient, hatch, plate wash). Do not bake paper into the motif file.
7. **Margin is composition.** A Haeckel plate is mostly paper. A moth that fills the frame is a stamp, not a specimen. Density is a taste choice, not a flex.

## Organism law

- One physics body per creature. Wings and spine are decorations.
- Bilateral is a reflected `<use>`, not a second simulation.
- Stamp is post-sim. Mirrored *worlds* are bake/FINAL only.
- The frame is a dish wall in hype. Swarm may wrap. Do not mix those without a name.
- Phrase wrap resets *phase*, never asset identity.

## Material law

- `--ink` and `--accent` are the only live pigments.
- Patterns and gradients live in `<defs>`, indexed by `u`.
- Compounds are attachments. They never enter a blend ladder.

## Speed law

Measure before WASM. SoA before fewer features. If the moth is expensive, instance it; do not delete flap.

## What we will not become

- A mini-Illustrator in the live tab.
- A particle screensaver with 40 knobs.
- A gallery of AI plates spliced into the 137.
- A renderer that is honest only on the farm.

If a change violates a pillar, it is not Kinetic Curator — even if it looks cooler for a night.
