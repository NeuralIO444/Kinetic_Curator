## The defect, as proven against this tree

The X-RAY table (DEV > GOVERNOR X-RAY) has three cost columns. Traced to
their sources:

- **est** — the cost registry's declared `timeMs` per pass
  (`engine/kernel/costRegistry.mjs`). Author estimates by contract, and
  already under an "est" header. The alarming-looking values in the
  issue (pattern/blocks 60ms, pattern/picture 25ms) live here — they
  were never presented as measurements. No change needed.
- **measured** — `MEASURED_COSTS` from `gl/effects/measuredCosts.mjs`,
  a file generated by `npm run measure-costs`, stamped
  `MEASURED_AT = 2026-09-27`. Two real problems:
  1. **Provenance was dropped.** Each entry carries a `method`
     ('wall'/'hw' from the sweep, 'estimate' for hand-committed
     numbers), but `buildXray` extracted only `.ms`. Of the 44 entries,
     20 are NOT sweep measurements: `gl/spectrum-marks` was already
     marked `method: 'estimate'` (its comment says "an ESTIMATE, not a
     sweep"), and 19 more were committed by hand while carrying
     `method: 'wall'` — their own comments say so: fx/sharpen + fx/haze
     (#1022, "added by hand, not re-measured"), fx/dither (#1610),
     fx/riso (#2008), fx/stipple (#2038), the twelve math/* ops (#1010,
     "the committed 0.3 ms class number"), gl/contact-shadow (#1183,
     derived as 12.0× a run's median, never swept), and beast/trails
     (#1868, committed at a chosen 0.02 bound ~6× its direct 0.0034
     reading). All of them rendered in the measured column,
     indistinguishable from swept values — estimates presenting as
     measurements.
  2. **No age on the numbers.** The only date was the panel header
     ("Measurements from 9/27/2026" — the genuine sweep stamp, 58 days
     old at the stress session). Hand-added October rows silently
     inherited the September date.
- **working set** — the registry's `memoryBytes`, documented in
  costRegistry.mjs as "An estimate, not a measurement". The uniform
  16 MB is one 1080p RGBA16F FBO (1920×1080×8 B ≈ 16.6 MB) declared per
  pass. The column header carried no estimate label.

### Premise corrections to the issue's framing

- The uniform **0.3ms is not fabricated data**: it is the sweep's floor
  on the reference rig — most pure-ALU passes quantize to 0.3 ms/draw
  under the wall method, as the file's header and row comments document
  (the CI gate works on ratios to the median for exactly this reason).
  The dishonesty was provenance and labeling, not invented numbers.
- The header date was accurate as far as it went; the gap was that no
  number carried its own date or age, per ruling 2.
- Rows whose comments document an actual wall-method measurement event
  with relative standing (the accum/* floor rows #1805/#1807/#560,
  fx/gradientMap #2443, the renderer/resolve pair #740) stay classified
  as measured — a measurement happened; the floor is the file's
  quantization convention. The estimate line was drawn exactly where
  the file's own comments draw it: no measurement of the pass itself.

## What changed

- `app/src/gl/governorXray.mjs` — `buildXray` now threads provenance:
  each pass row gains `measuredKind` ('measured' | 'estimate' | null),
  `measuredAt`, and `measuredAgeDays`; the result also returns the stamp
  and age. Only 'measured' rows carry the date/age. New optional args
  `measuredAt` and `now` (the clock is passed in — the determinism lint
  law keeps `Date.now` out of gl/, and the panel supplies it from a
  state initializer to satisfy React purity). Existing row fields are
  unchanged; `governorEventLog` and `governorHostileScene` suites pass
  unmodified.
- `app/src/gl/effects/measuredCosts.mjs` — the 19 hand-added rows now
  carry `method: 'estimate'`, recording in the data what their comments
  already said. No `ms` value changed. (A future `npm run measure-costs`
  re-bless regenerates this file wholesale; swept values then become
  genuine measurements and the labels follow the data automatically.)
- `app/src/panels/GovernorXrayPanel.jsx` — measured cell: an estimate
  renders muted as `0.3ms est.` (title: "estimate — committed by hand,
  not measured by the sweep"); a measurement older than the current
  session renders with its age on the number, `0.3ms · 58d` (title
  carries the full date); a same-session measurement renders plain.
  The header now reads "Measurements from 9/27/2026 (58 days ago)" and
  explains the est. mark. The working-set column is headed
  "working set (est.)".
- `app/src/gl/governorXray.selfcheck.mjs` (new) + one manifest line —
  pins the law: classification is exact (estimate/measured/bare/missing),
  age is whole days on the row, same-session ages 0, no stamp → no age,
  the 20 hand-added ids must keep `method: 'estimate'` in the committed
  data, a full build over the real registry classifies every row by its
  entry's method with estimates dateless, and the panel source keeps
  the rendering contract.
