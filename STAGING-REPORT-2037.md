## What was built

Every KINETIC press (CHAOS roll, LOOK pass, VOICE pass, CURATOR pass) now also rolls for a birth, inside the same commit as the press itself:

- **The pure core — `app/src/engine/kineticBirths.mjs` (new).** `KINETIC_BIRTH_RATE = 1/8` (named constant, ruling 3), `KINETIC_BIRTH_HAND_SIZE = 3`, bounded draw walks. The roll, the triad draw and the birth are pure functions of the recorded conversation — the standing seed, the per-press counter (`kineticBirthPress`, session-only, poemDeal's discipline), the standing population and the room. No `Math.random`, no clocks. Parents are legalized and bred by the standing machinery (`sourceRecord` / `breedCreature`), so the triad law, the ancestry written at birth (#2030) and the divergent-freeze decline all hold by construction; every child is stamped with its cultivation note through the #1872 door (`cultivationNote` + `withCultivation`) and kept only when the note reads **viable** — the cultivate gate (ruling 4).
- **The pre-made hand (amendment).** `curator/birthLive.mjs` (new) reads the standing population from store state — banked creatures and stage-born children whose tiles stand enabled, plus assemblies from the enabled dealt pool wearing the room's standing look (`layoutParams.composition`) and voice (`kineticVoiceId`) — and derives the hand exactly when the population's fingerprint changes. The live loop notes the population as it assembles each frame (`gl/liveLoop.mjs`); derivation is fingerprint-gated, so a steady stage costs one population read and no breeding. The trio scores in the core order the hand and only order it: LOIS reads the child's cultivation cost/warnings, Davis its crossed/mutated share, TE its discrete completeness, plus the spec's room bias (the room's voice, then look). A hit births the top of the hand; a stale or empty hand falls back to breeding on the spot under the same gate.
- **Born on stage (ruling 5, first half).** The press site (`landBirth` in `state/slices/layoutSlice.js`, landPoem's sibling, fail-closed like the review wrap) appends `{ record, tile }` to the session-only `stageBirths` and enables the child's tile over the commit's dealt pool, so a re-deal never sweeps a newborn off. The tile rides the resolver pool beside the overlay via `poolWithBirths` (input assembly + the baker's pool in `liveLoop.mjs`), which is also what the take records — a kept take replays its births exactly. `stageBirths` is never serialized and is cleared by `applyProject`.
- **Banked only on a kept take (ruling 5, second half).** A new take section, `births` (`curator/birthRecord.mjs` tap + recorder, the davisShift pattern; registered as a named factory in `PerformanceButton.jsx`'s sections registry, preserving its pinned shape), records every resolved roll — the attempt, and the child whole when one is born. Stopping a take is the keep: `PerformanceButton` banks the section's children through the new `bankStageBirths` action, which goes through the bank's one door — `bankCreature` gained a stage-birth branch that moves the child's standing tile into the overlay as the record enters the bank and clears the stage-birth entry (same caps, same overlay door, same statuses). An unkept performance's children leave with it: a saved project carries no trace of an unbanked child (proven in the suite: no record, no tile, and the dangling enable is collapsed by the load path's own sanitizer).

## Scope notes and honest limits

- **The launch opener is not hooked.** `kineticOpenPass` (the START pill's WEAK/MED opener) is a launch pass, not a KINETIC button press; the four button actions are the press surface the issue's rulings describe ("every KINETIC press"). If Matt wants the opener to roll too, it is a one-line follow-up through the same `landBirth`.
- **CHAOS reads the standing conversation.** A CHAOS press deals a new seed in its own commit; the birth resolves against the state as the press begins (standing seed + press number + standing stage), and the child lands in the new deal. The take still replays exactly, because the recorded inputs carry the merged pool.
- **FLAGGED DESIGN QUESTION — retro-kept takes do not bank.** The deliberate take carries the `births` section and banks on keep. The always-on retro ring records inputs only (no sections), so a retro-saved take replays its births on stage but cannot name them for banking. The spec's ruling 5 speaks of "the take it was born in"; whether a retro save counts as that take being kept is not answered by the issue, the spec, or any standing ruling, so I did not guess. Options when it comes up: (a) stamp births with a ring-frame ordinal and bank the in-window ones on retro save; (b) rule retro saves out of banking (status quo). The deliberate-take path — the ruled surface — is complete either way.
- **Undo leaves a dormant entry.** Undoing a birth press restores the pre-press enabled map (the child leaves the stage) but `stageBirths` is session state outside the undo stacks, so the entry remains, inert: not drawn (tile disabled), not a parent (the population reads enabled tiles), bankable only by the take that actually recorded its birth. It leaves with the session or the next project load.
- **Exports/stills** resolve from the document, so an unbanked child does not appear in a still baked mid-performance; it appears in the live stage and in takes, which is where it lives until banked.
- **Assemblies need a standing room.** Before any voice pass has set `kineticVoiceId`, assemblies are unavailable (the breeder's door requires the room's own look + voice), so the earliest presses of a fresh session can only birth from on-stage creatures. That is the standing source law, not a new restriction.
